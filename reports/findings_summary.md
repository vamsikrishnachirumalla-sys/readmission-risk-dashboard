# Findings Summary (Sprint 2)

Prepared for: Director of Quality

**Overall 30-day readmission rate: 9.0%** across 69,987 patients.

## What stands out

1. **Repeat hospital users are the clearest high-risk group.** Patients with 3+ inpatient stays in the past year were readmitted at 26.5%, versus 8.1% for patients with none.
2. **Discharged to skilled nursing or other facility** has the highest relative risk: 15.4% vs 7.7% (2.0x).
3. **Injury** is the highest-risk primary diagnosis group at 10.8% (1.20x the overall rate).
4. **Stays of 8+ days** have the highest readmission rate at 11.8%.

## What this means for care managers

Start discharge follow-up with patients who were already in the hospital in the past year. That one question catches a big share of the highest-risk group.

## Caveats

These are associations, not causes. Sicker patients both stay longer and come back more often, so length of stay is partly standing in for how sick someone is. Sprint 3 looks at all the factors together.
